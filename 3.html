Healthy Eating Tips

🥦 Eat Variety

Include different fruits, vegetables, grains and protein foods.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day.

🌾 Choose Whole Grains

Try oats, brown rice and whole-wheat foods.

🍽️ Enjoy Balanced Meals

Include a mix of food groups and eat according to your needs.
